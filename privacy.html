Clash Samurai privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Clash Samurai is a free, unofficial companion app for Clash of Clans players. It is built to work without collecting anything about you.

What the app collects

Nothing. Clash Samurai has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not read your contacts, location, photos, clipboard or any identifier of your device.

What stays on your device

Your saved layouts and strategies, your default Town Hall, your theme choice and your last-used spell levels are stored only in the app's local storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network use

On launch and when you check for updates, the app downloads content files (layout lists, strategy guides, glossary terms and calculator tables) and layout preview images from https://benjirank.github.io/clashsamurai-content/, a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log the IP address of that request as part of operating its service; see GitHub's privacy statement. Clash Samurai never sends anything about you with those requests.

Links to other apps and sites

Tapping "Copy to Clash of Clans" or "Copy Army" opens an official link.clashofclans.com link in the Clash of Clans app. Tapping a builder credit, "Rate Clash Samurai" or "Send feedback" opens your browser, the app store or your mail app. Those apps and sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new date.
